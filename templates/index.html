Industry-Grade Features

🧠 Time-Series Intelligence

Lag features (1hr, 24hr) and rolling averages for true time-series awareness — real predictive intelligence, not just pattern matching.

⚡ Peak/Off-Peak Detection

Automatic identification of peak demand hours (6–10 AM, 6–11 PM) for load scheduling and dynamic pricing optimization.

🌡️ Temperature Index

Cooling (>24°C) and heating (<18°C) indices model human behaviour, not just raw temperature data.

📅 Holiday Impact Analysis

Indian public holiday detection (2023–2026) with 15–30% demand reduction modelling for accurate nationwide forecasting.

📊 Trend Detection

24-hour and 7-day rolling averages smooth noise and identify long-term demand patterns for grid stability planning.

🔮 What-If Scenarios

Compare two prediction scenarios side-by-side — different temperatures, dates, or hours — to optimise grid planning decisions.
